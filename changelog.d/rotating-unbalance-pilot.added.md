- `pinneapple_analysis.inspection.rotating`: order tracking from a once-per-revolution tachometer (shaft angle, speed, complex phasor of each order referred to the pulse, order resolution of the record); an index and order features of the MAFAULDA machinery-fault database; and the calibrated inversion of the 1X response for the unbalance mass across speeds (influence coefficients as complex splines, a linear dissipative modal model with positive damping, the classical phase-free calibration, a 1X phase that may change between recording sessions). `unbalance_study.run_pilot` compares them under fixed criteria, at seen speeds and in held-out speed bands; the example is `examples/mafaulda_unbalance_pilot/pilot.py` (needs the MAFAULDA download). On MAFAULDA the decision is NO GO for phase-referenced force reconstruction: the magnitude of the unbalance follows the added mass, but the 1X phase reference drifts between mass levels, and the classical phase-free calibration stays the most accurate.
- `pinneapple_analysis.inspection.rotating`: amplitude and envelope order spectra on a fixed order grid (`order_spectrum`, computed order tracking by `angle_resample`); a tachometer that rejects spikes (minimum pulse width, refractory period from the spectral fundamental) and flags runs whose pulses are not one per revolution; a channel-screened mass estimate (`ScreenedEstimator`, Tukey biweight of the per-channel estimates started from their median) that rejects an anomalous accelerometer; `unbalance_study.cross_session` for calibration on some recording sessions and test on others.
