- `pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.dc_microgrid` and the Flow node `twin.dc_microgrid`: a digital twin of a four-bus DC microgrid (two droop converters, a PV converter in MPPT, a resistive and a constant-power load, 13 sensors) that tells a changed network (a loose joint in a line, a PV converter losing efficiency, a droop converter's output resistance growing) from a drifting or noisy sensor from a fast model outside its domain. It uses 13 physics residuals (Kirchhoff, Ohm, droop, MPPT and load laws), whitened by the propagated sensor noise, and a one-sided generalised likelihood ratio per fault hypothesis; a Kalman filter on the fault sizes; isolability angles over the envelope (which faults look alike, and where); and a physics-informed fast model trained with `train_graybox`, compared with a data-only network and POD + GP at equal budget, inside and outside the envelope. The study ends in a trust report (`run_study`); the example is `examples/dc_microgrid_twin/microgrid_twin.py`. The plant is simulated, with assumed converter and sensor data.
