\section*{Highlights}
% Elsevier: 3 to 5 bullet points, at most 85 characters each (including spaces).
\begin{itemize}
\item Fault classifiers above 0.97 macro-F1 lose 0.11--0.32 at unseen rotation speeds
\item A leakage-free, speed-held-out protocol for MAFAULDA and COMFAULDA
\item A random forest on order features is the most accurate and stable model overall
\item A speed-conditioned Fourier neural operator is best for unbalance (0.93 vs 0.85)
\item Compound faults are not recognised from single-fault training (exact match 5\%)
\end{itemize}
